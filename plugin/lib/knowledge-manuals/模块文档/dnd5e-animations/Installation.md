Upon opening the world with the module enabled, and consequently with every D&D5e Animations update, you will be prompted to update your animations as follows.
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/32039708/221057091-5a239d50-b9cb-4158-a862-c34517eca04c.png)

Press **Update** to update your animations.

If you wish to open the menu again, you can do so in `Module Settings > D&D5e Animations > Update Menu`
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/32039708/221057253-4b981c13-c0e4-4088-a3d7-302482512df7.png)
